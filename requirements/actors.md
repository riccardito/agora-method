# Actors

The catalogue's actor vocabulary. A story's `actor:` must be one of these headings (rule C06).

### developer

Works with the AI agent in sessions: opens them, nods at gates, closes them.

### maintainer

Keeps the skills, policies and catalogue true; owns the checks and their baselines.

### requirements-engineer

Writes and reviews the catalogue; owns the traceability from story to test.

### reader

Reads the repository from outside, without access to the parent project.
